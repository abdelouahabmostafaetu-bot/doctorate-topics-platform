Exercise of the day

Let E be a Banach space and let (Tn) be a sequence in 𝓛(E, E) such that for every x ∈ E the sequence (Tnx) converges. Define Tx = lim Tnx.

‖T‖ ≤ lim infn→∞ ‖Tn‖ < +∞
  1. (1) Show that supn ‖Tn‖ < +∞.
  2. (2) Deduce that T ∈ 𝓛(E, E) and prove the estimate above.
  3. (3) Give a counterexample when E is not complete.
